Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after I submit? ▼
You'll get a free assessment checklist via email and be introduced to 2-3 local roofers in your area. They'll contact you directly to schedule a free inspection. No obligation.
Do you charge for this? ▼
No, the assessment and contractor matching are 100% free. You only pay if you hire someone for repairs.
How fast will they contact me? ▼
Most contractors respond within 24 hours. During busy season (spring/summer), often same-day.
Can you help with insurance claims? ▼
Yes. Most contractors help file claims and navigate the process. Ask your contractor about their claims support.
