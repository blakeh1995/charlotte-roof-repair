Storm Damage Checklist: How to Inspect Your Roof After Severe Weather

A storm just hit Charlotte. Your roof's been through it. Now what? Here's your room-by-room and roof inspection checklist before you call your insurer.

Inside Your Home: Signs of Roof Damage

Check ceilings and walls for water stains (fresh spots, not old ones)
Look in corners and along edges where roof meets walls
Check attic insulation for wet spots or water streaks
Smell for musty odors (early sign of moisture)
Look at light fixtures—water around them suggests roof leak above

From the Ground: Visual Roof Inspection

Don't go on the roof yet. Use binoculars from the ground and check:

Missing shingles (dark patches where shingles were)
Curled or lifted shingles
Dents in the roof (hail damage shows as round depressions)
Debris on the roof (branches, parts of gutters, etc.)
Damaged gutters or downspouts
Damaged flashing around chimneys, vents, or skylights

In the Gutters

Look at what's in them:

Granules (sand-like material from shingles) = shingle damage
Debris from roof penetrations (vent caps, flashing pieces)
Gutters themselves bent, pulled away, or damaged

Exterior Walls & Siding

Damage to siding, trim, fascia boards
Cracks in caulking or sealant around windows and doors
Dents in metal gutters or downspouts

After You've Inspected

Document everything with photos and video. Timestamp them (they'll show the date). Write down what you found. This becomes your damage report before the insurance adjuster arrives.

Don't repair or remove anything yet. Your insurer may want to see the damage. Let them verify it first, then proceed with repairs.
