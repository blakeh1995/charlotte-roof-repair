Roof Leak Repair: How to Spot and Fix Water Damage in Charlotte

Water stains on your ceiling. A damp smell in the attic. Those are the first signs a roof leak is costing you money every time it rains. Here's what homeowners in Charlotte need to know.

Where Leaks Start

Most roof leaks don't come from a hole in shingles. They come from where things meet: where the roof meets the chimney, where a vent sticks through, where two roof planes intersect. These "flashings" are metal trim that's supposed to seal the gap. If the sealant cracks or the flashing rusts, water finds its way in.

Shingles fail too. Curling shingles (usually after 10+ years), torn shingles from wind, or missing shingles from hail—any of these give water a path underneath.

Early Signs (Before Water Inside)

  • Granules in gutters: Asphalt shingles shed granules as they age. If you see gravel in your gutters, the shingles are breaking down.
  • Sagging spots: A dip in the roofline means water's pooling and the decking underneath is softening.
  • Moss or algae: Common in Charlotte's humidity. It traps water against the shingles.
  • Lifted shingles: Wind or age can lift the edges. Water sneaks underneath.

Water's Already Inside: What to Do

Ceiling stains. Wet insulation in the attic. A musty smell. Don't wait.

First, find where the water's entering. Go into the attic on a rainy day with a flashlight. Follow the wet spot upward—the leak is usually 3-5 feet up the slope from where the water's dripping. Mark it with a piece of tape.

Second, stop new water from entering. If it's raining, that means temporary: put a tarp over the damaged area, secure it, get it done today. A leak costs $300-800 just in water damage inside your home for every week it sits.

Third, document everything. Take photos for your insurance claim. Note the date. Get a contractor out for a professional assessment.

Repair Costs

Small patch (isolated shingle damage): $250-400 in parts and labor.

Flashing replacement (chimney or vent): $450-850 per penetration.

Larger leak with interior damage: $800-2,000 depending on what needs drying/replacing.

If the leak's been there for months, you might have rotting wood in the decking. That's structural work, and costs climb fast.

Water Damage? Act Now

Leaks get worse in rain. Get a professional assessment within 24 hours to prevent mold and structural damage.

Insurance & Leaks

Your homeowners policy covers sudden damage from storms (wind, hail, fallen branches). It usually does NOT cover gradual wear—a leak from a roof that's just old.

If a storm caused the damage, you have 30 days to file a claim. Call your insurance company, don't wait for a contractor to tell you to. You want their adjuster to see the damage before weather makes it worse.

Prevention

Inspect your roof twice a year: spring and fall. Look from the ground with binoculars first—no need to climb. Check for curling, missing shingles, and debris. Clean gutters after leaves fall. Poor drainage causes leaks.

If your roof's over 12 years old in Charlotte's heat, get a professional inspection. You might be looking at replacement soon anyway.
