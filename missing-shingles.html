Missing Shingles: Quick Repair Guide for Charlotte Homeowners

One, three, or a dozen shingles gone. Whether it's wind, age, or a tree branch, missing shingles need replacing fast. Water will find any gap.

Why Shingles Go Missing

  • Wind: Strong winds lift shingles, especially at edges. Once lifted, they tear off.
  • Age: After 10+ years, the adhesive fails and shingles loosen. First big wind takes them.
  • Debris: Tree branches, hail, other impacts can tear shingles right off.
  • Poor installation: Under-nailed shingles come loose first.

How Bad Is It?

A few missing shingles: Patch repair, $300-600. A contractor replaces the damaged shingles and seals them down.

Missing shingles across a whole roof section: This suggests your roof's at end-of-life. Consider replacement estimates.

Temporary Fix (Until Contractor Arrives)

Don't go on the roof if you're not comfortable. Put a tarp over the area or use roofing tape to seal open spots. It'll hold for a few days, but it's not permanent.

The Repair

A contractor will:

  • Remove damaged shingles
  • Check the underlayment and decking underneath for damage
  • Install new matching shingles (color match matters)
  • Seal and nail properly so they don't blow off again

Cost: $250-600 for a few shingles. Labor is the bulk of it.

Prevention

If your roof's 10+ years old, missing shingles are a sign. Trim tree branches overhanging the roof. Clean gutters so water doesn't pool and loosen shingles further.
