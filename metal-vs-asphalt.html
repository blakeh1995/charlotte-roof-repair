Asphalt: The Standard Choice

Pros:

Cheap upfront. Familiar—most roofers know it. Wide color and style options. Quiet. Easy to repair spot damage.

Cons:

Short lifespan in Charlotte's heat and humidity. Hail damage is expensive to repair. Needs cleaning every few years. Granules degrade over time.

Best for:

Budget-conscious homeowners, folks who plan to move in 10-15 years, or homeowners in hail-light areas.

Metal: The Long-Term Play

Pros:

Lasts 40-50 years—you probably won't replace it again. Survives hail without damage. Minimal maintenance. Energy efficient (reflects heat). Recyclable.

Cons:

2-3x more expensive. Can be noisy in rain (unless properly insulated). Limited color options (though improving). Some HOAs restrict metal roofs. Requires specialized contractors.

Best for:

Long-term homeowners, Charlotte hail corridor residents, people willing to pay more for durability.

The Charlotte Factor: Hail

We get 14-20 hail events per year. Asphalt gets hammered and costs money to repair every few years. Metal takes dents but doesn't leak. If you're in Charlotte 10+ years, metal's ROI makes sense despite higher upfront cost.

Bottom Line

Asphalt if you're tight on budget or plan to move. Metal if you're staying put and want a roof that outlasts your mortgage.
