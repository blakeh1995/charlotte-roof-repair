Roof Replacement Cost: What You'll Pay in Charlotte

Your roof's at end-of-life. Insurance declined to repair. You're looking at replacement. Here's what it costs in Charlotte and what affects the price.

Asphalt Shingle Roof

Average Cost: $6,000 - $12,000

For a typical 2,000 sq ft home (30 roofing squares)

Price per square foot: $3-6 installed

Asphalt is the cheapest and most common. You're getting a 15-20 year roof in Charlotte's climate, longer if you maintain it.

Metal Roof

Average Cost: $15,000 - $30,000

For the same 2,000 sq ft home

Price per square foot: $8-15 installed

Metal costs 2-3x more upfront but lasts 40-50 years. For Charlotte hail, it's worth considering if you're staying in the home long-term.

What Affects the Price?

  • Roof pitch (steepness): Steep roofs cost more—more labor, more materials, higher danger
  • Roof complexity: Gables, valleys, skylights, chimneys = higher cost
  • Old material removal: If roofers have to tear off old roof, add $1-3 per sq ft
  • Decking damage: If old plywood is rotted, it needs replacing. $5-15 per sq ft extra
  • Ventilation/upgrades: Better ventilation or premium shingles cost more
  • Timing: Spring and summer (busy season) = higher prices. Winter: lower

Your Actual Out-of-Pocket Cost

If insurance covers it: You pay the deductible ($500-2,000), insurance covers the rest.

If insurance doesn't cover it: You pay the full amount. Some contractors offer financing (0% interest for 12 months, etc.). Ask about it.

Get Multiple Quotes

Price varies wildly. A $6,000 quote doesn't mean another contractor is wrong at $9,000—different materials, warranties, labor practices. Get 2-3 quotes, compare what's included:

  • Removal of old roof and disposal
  • Replacement decking (if needed)
  • Ventilation upgrades
  • Warranty on materials and labor (5 year? 25 year?)
  • Cleanup and debris removal

Financing Your Replacement

If insurance doesn't cover it, you have options:

  • Contractor financing (0% for 12 months common)
  • Home equity line of credit (HELOC)
  • Personal loan from your bank
  • Pay out-of-pocket if you can

Don't rush a cheapest quote. A $4,000 roof is cheaper for a reason. You get what you pay for.
